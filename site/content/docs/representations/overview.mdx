## Core forms

| Representation | Use it for | Main controls |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `cartoon` | protein or polymer context | `width`, `style` |
| `ball_and_stick` | local contacts and bonds | `radius`, `bond_radius` |
| `spacefill` | atomic volume | `radius` |
| `licorice` | uniform-radius atoms and bonds | `radius`, `bond_radius` |
| `lines` | compact bond overview | `width` |
| `points` | dense atom clouds | `size` |
| `surface` | molecular envelope | `kind`, `style`, `probe_radius`, `isolevel` |

All forms accept `target`, `opacity`, and `color`. `nucleic_acid`, `bases`, `base_pairs`, and `glycan` are also available for their respective molecular targets.

```python
scene.add(molgfx.rep.cartoon(target="protein", color=molgfx.color.secondary_structure()))
scene.add(molgfx.rep.licorice(target="resname HEM", bond_radius=0.15))
```

For ball-and-stick, licorice, and lines, call `structure.infer_bonds()` after reading mmCIF when the source does not contain all bonds.
